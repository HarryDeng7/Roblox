What is Roblox?

A platform, not a game.

Most games are one world with one set of rules. Roblox is one app that opens into millions of different experiences — all made by players, for players. If you've ever thought "I could make this cooler," Roblox is built for exactly that thought.

One app, millions of experiences

Everything on Roblox is an experience: obstacle courses, tycoons, racing games, horror maps, hangout worlds. New ones appear every single day — you could play a different game every hour for years and never run out.

Players are the creators

Every experience was built by someone — many of them kids and teens who started out playing, then opened Roblox Studio and learned to build. Today's players really do become tomorrow's creators.

Play together, anywhere

Roblox runs on PC, Mac, phones, tablets and consoles. Friends jump into the same world, at the same time, in real time — whether they're in the same room or on the other side of the planet.

How Roblox works

Two sides of one platform.

Roblox runs on a simple loop: most people come to play, and plenty of them go on to build. Pick a side to see how it works.

  1. 1

    Create an account & an avatar

    Signing up is free. Your avatar is you in every experience — change its clothes, accessories and colors whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Browse millions of experiences

    Pick from obbies, tycoons, adventures, racing games and way more. New experiences land every single day.

  3. 3

    Play with friends on live servers

    Join the same world as your friends and play together in real time — from any device, anywhere.

  4. 4

    Collect badges, items & Robux

    Wins, unlocks and avatar items stack up as you go. Some experiences even pay out Robux to their players.

  1. 1

    Download Roblox Studio

    A free app for Windows and Mac. Same Roblox account, brand-new superpower: making instead of playing.

  2. 2

    Build your world with parts

    Snap colorful blocks together into terrain, buildings and obstacles. Think digital LEGO with infinite bricks — no 3D modeling skills needed.

  3. 3

    Script the fun with Lua

    A few lines of Roblox's Lua-based code make things happen: lava rises, coins spin, doors open. Guided tutorials explain every line.

  4. 4

    Test & publish with one click

    Hit play to test your game instantly, then publish it so anyone on Roblox can join — that's millions of potential players.

  5. 5

    Grow your game and your skills

    Listen to player feedback, ship updates, and watch your experience grow. Popular creators can even earn Robux for their work.

Here's the best part of the loop: every builder started as a player. The only thing between "playing Roblox" and "building on Roblox" is opening Roblox Studio for the first time — and that's exactly the leap Build Day helps you make.

Where games come from

Roblox Studio: the free game-making tool.

Every experience you've ever played on Roblox was made in Roblox Studio — a free app for Windows and Mac that anyone can download with their normal Roblox account.

You start with simple blocks and terrain. When you're ready, Roblox's Lua-based language adds the magic: lava that rises, doors that open, coins that count. Test-play instantly, then publish to the whole world with one click.

  • Free to download
  • Drag-and-drop building
  • Lua-based scripting
  • Instant test play
  • Publish to millions

The big idea

Interesting to play. Useful for life.

Roblox is more than a way to pass the time. It's a place where fun and real skills live in the same world — that's what makes it worth knowing about.

Interesting: it never gets old

  • Millions of experiences — from 30-second quickies to 100-hour adventures
  • New games and updates appear every day
  • Play with friends wherever they are, in the same world at the same time
  • Your avatar, your style, your way to play

Useful: building teaches skills that stick

  • Game design thinking: levels, rules, and what makes things fun
  • Coding basics in Lua — real logic and real problem-solving
  • Teamwork: planning together, sharing credit, giving feedback
  • A head start: creators can earn Robux, and game design is a real career path

That's our big idea in one line: Roblox is interesting to play and useful to learn from. Playing is great — building is your next level.
