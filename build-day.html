What is Roblox Build Day?

A hackathon, but built for beginners.

Hackathons are events where people build something from scratch — usually in one rushed weekend. Build Day takes that idea, spreads it over a whole week, and makes it 100% online and 100% beginner-friendly.

Brand-new to Roblox? Start with the plain-English guide: What is Roblox & how it works.

A challenge theme

Every Build Day kicks off with a creative theme. Your mission: make a game that brings it to life — an obby, a tycoon, a mystery... you decide.

Learn as you build

No boring lectures. Short tutorials, tips, and example games teach you Roblox Studio while you make your own game, step by step.

Submit & celebrate

Turn in your game on Friday and show it off in our online showcase, where builders from around the world share what they made.

Who is it for?

Made for players. Trusted by parents.

How the week works

One week. Five steps. Your first game.

Everything happens online and at your own pace. There's just one deadline: submit your game by Friday at 5 PM.

Day 1Monday

Challenge drop

The theme is revealed. Install Roblox Studio (it's free), join the event hub, and meet your fellow builders.

Day 2Tuesday

Learn the ropes

Short beginner tutorials show you how to place parts, shape terrain, and write your very first lines of Lua.

Day 3Wednesday

Build your game

Time to create! Build your experience with step-by-step guides, tips, and example games to borrow ideas from.

Day 4Thursday

Polish & playtest

Add sounds and effects, squash bugs, and playtest with friends until your game feels fun to play.

Day 5Friday

Submit & celebrate

Submit before 5 PM, then join the online showcase where everyone shows off what they built this week.

What you'll build & learn

Finish the week as a game creator.

You'll end Build Day with a playable Roblox experience that you designed and scripted yourself — maybe an obby, a racing game, a tycoon, or whatever the theme sparks.

And even if it's quick and dirty (that's the hackathon spirit!), you'll walk away with skills that stick around long after the week is over.
